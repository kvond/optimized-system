## A. Reconcile and produce reports

scope at a time. A hold in one scope stays there; it does not certify or block all
other verified scopes. Complete required source/history evidence for that scope
is still mandatory. A whole-class missing-work claim needs whole-class evidence.

- Read the current class roster, exact Agenda/Form lesson identity, submitted
  original and revision, and saved assessment/report history. Keep distinct
  lessons separate even when they share a week, folder or IC same-grade group.
  A blank sheet cell means no located link, not no submission or no feedback.
- Locate and link existing valid reports before generating anything. Compare the
  actual reviewed source/version; a newer filename or PDF date does not prove
  newer student work was assessed. Reuse unchanged reviewed reports, including
  Claude's saved catch-up work, without another assessment or duplicate file.

  feedback and science-writing assessment where applicable using
  `skills/student-feedback` (the canonical feedback prompt, scale and class
  formats; the Agenda Feedback Prompts tab is Katherine's quick reference), the
  teaching manual and the approved exemplar reports the skill locates. Read those
  models, not just their filenames. Keep the
  two judgments distinct. Preserve attributed student evidence, substantive explanation, useful graphics,
  accessible practice and writing space from the established formats. Do not
  invent quotations, scores, improvement, source dates or a writing sample.
  Unassessed writing remains unassessed; N/A is not a zero or Beginning.
  Critical-aspect reports retain the approved focused question, reflection and

## B. Build the printable file independently

Always evaluate `school-feedback-packet-assembly` separately, including when A is
held. It uses saved verified reports and the frozen spreadsheet selection; it
requires neither generation capability nor outbox, Hub or IC availability.
Do not weaken A's source gates to make B pass. Unavailable current intake means
the packet is latest verified/available coverage, not proof that all new work is
reviewed. Do not package a document another writer is still changing.

Produce or reuse ONE dated printable PDF, grouped by class and student, containing
each current student's most recent reviewed feedback and writing report/scores.
Choose by actual verified submitted/reviewed source chronology, not highest week
or newest file modification. If the two report types concern different pieces,
show each lesson and submission date. Include the actual feedback, not just a
score/link list. Preserve earlier individual files and dated packets. Exclude
teacher-only analyses, drafts and only proven duplicate representations.

The immutable packet record identifies the exact included report versions and
page ranges, PDF ID/hash, build date and omitted/held scopes. Reopen the complete
saved PDF, check bytes, page order, readable graphics, margins and page breaks,
and verify the private destination. Publish its working link prominently in this
same spreadsheet, then update the existing Hub link when available. Hub failure
does not invalidate the saved spreadsheet/PDF result. Never label a partial packet
as every latest submission completed; one held student does not prevent a clearly
labeled packet of the other verified reports.

## C. Make status visible without manual bookkeeping

Derive colours from report-version and packet-inclusion records, not from reading
cell paint. Yellow means a saved verified report version is not yet included in a
verified packet. White means that exact version is in a saved, reopened, checked
packet. A newer version becomes yellow again. An interrupted/failed packet build
does not clear yellow. Missing feedback, N/A, unsubmitted and unavailable sources
remain explicit statuses; a blank/white cell is not proof of completion.

An IC check requires the exact existing helper's confirmed student/assignment/
component/score readback and date, or a separately authorized equivalent exact
IC read. Keep higher retained scores identifiable. Do not infer IC entry from a
report score, packet, helper readiness, old whole-assignment stamp or checkbox.
Unverified components stay unverified; this task never enters IC grades or creates
an IC assignment. Packet inclusion and IC verification are independent.

The spreadsheet's `IC check` tab (added 2026-10-21 at Katherine's request) reads
the Hub's `Scores entered` record live through one IMPORTRANGE and lists each IC
assignment (Form ID) with its latest helper readback, the helper's per-section
entered/raised/left counts, any hand mark from the Hub and a status. It updates
itself; do not repaint, rewrite or duplicate it. It is assignment-level evidence:
"left" scores still need Katherine's hand check, and it does not confirm an
individual student's score. Lesson columns are not bound to IC assignments there,
because students often turn a deck in through another week's Form.
