# Continuity lane

You own the people, the scenes, and the pointers: that the greenhouse student has one name in every chapter, that Marcus's 4 a.m. is narrated once, that "the next chapter" is the next chapter, that a scene told in Chapter 5 is recalled in The Moves rather than retold. You do not own definitions of terms (terms), the seating of claims (claims), or what a scholar said (notes).

Read first: `book-revision/SKILL.md`; `decisions.md` (R11, R13, R16, R18; P1, P3, P8, P9, P10, P19, P22); the name-change log in `revision/reference/rulings-from-register-2026-08.md` (book repo); the continuity pass in `reference/findings/`, sections A2, A3, A8, A9, B, C, E. Then the chapter, with the checker's pseudonym table, cross-reference list, and shared-runs list beside it.

## Procedure, per chapter

1. **People.** For every pseudonym in the chapter, confirm against the checker's table and the name-change log who the person is and where they were introduced. A person used before the reader meets them gets a `bracket` row proposing one introducing clause at the first mention (or a pointer to the chapter that introduces them). A person with two names gets `rename` rows under the ruling (R18: Peter is the greenhouse student, Paul is the son, Eli is retired; Maya is Nancy, Joe is John, Mekhi is Malik; Peter Gray is never renamed) with every Find string. A name that is in neither the checker's list nor the log (Tyaire, Jaylen, Theo, Diego, Jair, Christian in the practice chapter) gets one `open-item` row: she confirms it is a pseudonym. A fact stated two ways (the greenhouse student's identity; two sons transferring after homeschooling; The Circle School's age; Kalima for Zara; Frieda and Freida) gets one row each.

2. **Scenes told twice.** For each pair in the checker's shared-runs list involving this chapter with three or more shared eight-word runs, one row: which chapter is the home (the ledger says for Marcus, Ben, the greenhouse, Lezir; otherwise the chapter that first needs the scene keeps the full telling), and what the other chapter keeps (a one-sentence recall in her words, drawn from the copy, class `relocate`, or a pointer, class `bracket`). Deliberate returns exist (project memory: Marcus has two bookend passages); the row says when a repetition is a return and should stand, and the checker's count is not by itself a verdict.

3. **Chapter 11 (P10).** The Moves copied whole scenes from Chapters 5, 6A, and 7 on 2026-09-12 (380, 358, and 245 shared runs). For each section of Chapter 11, one row: the sentences that are the move and its guard (they stay), the sentences that retell a scene from another chapter (they become a callback: one sentence of hers from the copy plus a chapter pointer), with the Find string of the first line of the copied block and its home chapter.

4. **Pointers (P22).** Every "Chapter N", "next chapter", "last chapter", "two chapters from now", "later in the book", "opened this chapter", "set aside in the Introduction" in the chapter: confirm it resolves under the ledger's architecture (P22: 5 Dialogic Teaching, 6 Before Inquiry, 7 the AI chapter, 8 the Room, 9 Authorial, 10 whole school, 11 Holding the Tension, 12 The Moves, 13 capstone) and points at something the target contains. The checker lists them; the continuity pass, section B, lists the ones known to fail. A wrong number is `fix-direct` when the target is unambiguous under P22; a pointer at content the target lacks is a `bracket` (say what the target does contain, or where the content lives). "Chapter 6" claims in The Moves (entries on the fiddle sim, the student moves, the Toxicofera exchange) resolve to the practice chapter or the AI chapter under R16 and P19; say which.

5. **The three claimants to "Chapter 11".** Nussbaum's capabilities (Chapter 3, twice), Rogoff and Lave & Wenger in full (the Room chapter), the moves: under P22, the first two point to the capstone (13) and the third to The Moves (12). Rows accordingly.

5a. **The practice chapter (R16, P22).** Its scenes and the four sections it shares with the AI chapter (the dialogic register, the skull station, two things per class, double booking) get one home each under P22's default, with a recall sentence at the other; its Lezir/Toxicofera opening is the home of that scene under P9 (Chapter 5 points forward, the AI chapter and The Moves point back); the Germantown and Montessori returns in it are deliberate returns unless the run count says a paragraph was copied whole. The earlier draft (`revision/reference/ch05-earlier-draft-2026-05.md`) is a source for what is missing, not a second telling: where it has a passage the Aug file lacks, the row is `relocate` with the passage quoted; where both have it, the Aug file's wording stands.

6. **Case facts.** Nancy is John's fourth-grader in a research classroom, not Katherine's student: the two Chapter 6 sentences that make her Katherine's get `bracket` rows (`with Nancy it was accidental`; `When Nancy says something`). Nancy's arrangement is aesthetic and geometric everywhere but Chapter 6A, where it becomes the classmates' fairness comparison: a `bracket` row. The classmates are "a paradigmatic difference" in Chapter 1 and sense-making in Chapters 3 and 5: one row, the challenge in brackets (both cannot stand). The Circle School, "the Sudbury-model school I researched", and "The Socratic School": rows that make the first two one school by name and keep the third distinct, with the "absurdist" attribution flagged for her (`open-item: which site`). The wrestler in Chapter 10 and the skull station in 6B: rows (no wrestler exists in the book; the skull station lives in the assembled Chapter 6 and needs a pointer or a first description).

7. **Lezir (P9).** Rows applying the default: Chapter 5's [GAP] closes with a pointer to the 6A scene; Chapter 6B's "that Tuesday" stands; Chapter 11's callback points to 6A. If she overrules, the rows say which lines change instead.
