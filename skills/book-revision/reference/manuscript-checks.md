# Manuscript-Specific Checks

These checks apply to the Answerable Teaching manuscript alongside the voice guide and tics-list. They were developed during the September 2026 revision session and reflect the current state of the argument.

## 1. Student Visibility (Voice Guide Marker 15)

Every chapter that discusses dialogic methods must name the student-visibility gap. Dialogic methods assume the student is already willing to speak. The first three movements create the conditions that precede dialogue. Without them, forced talk produces pseudo-agency.

If a chapter discusses dialogue without this critique, flag it.

## 2. Move Name Consistency

Every move name must match the section headers in Chapter 11 (The Moves) exactly:

1. Say what you see before you ask what it means
2. Alongside, not over
3. Lower the floor without lowering the thought
4. Excavate when nothing has surfaced; Hear the first real question as a bid
5. Write the interior without forcing publicity; Name the frame in the interrogative
6. Move effortful students from completion to meaning
7. Read absence as absent standing, not absent capacity
8. Expect self-direction to appear before it holds
9. Hear complaints as democratic openings
10. Teach the student-side moves
11. Survive the observation without hollowing the practice
12. Bank cases, but never finalize the child

Common drift: "receive the bid" should be "hear the first real question as a bid." "Bringing the contrast" is not a separate move (folded into move 1). "Protecting orientation time" is folded into move 3.

## 3. Theory-Movements-Moves Pipeline

The five movements must connect explicitly to the classroom moves in Chapter 11:

| Movement | Theoretical Source | Classroom Move |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The perceptible object | Variation Theory (Marton and Booth) | Say what you see before you ask what it means |
| 2. Joint attention and proximity | Vygotsky | Alongside, not over |
| 3. Legitimate peripheral participation | Lave and Wenger; Ito et al. | Lower the floor without lowering the thought |
| 4. Dialogic answerability | Bakhtin; Matusov | Excavate when nothing has surfaced; Hear the first real question as a bid |
| 5. Graduated transfer | (synthesized) | Write the interior without forcing publicity; Name the frame in the interrogative |

If a chapter discusses theory without connecting it to a movement or a classroom move, flag it. Chapter 13 (The Apprenticeship) is the exception: it makes the pipeline explicit as its central purpose.

## 4. Causal Overclaim Check

Flag phrases that assert causation beyond the evidence:
- "produces" suggest "can produce" or "often produces"
- "never" suggest "often" or "in the cases examined"
- "by design" suggest "is likely to reinforce"
- "the system has colonized you" suggest "may be experienced as an internalized accountability voice"
- "coverage produces pseudo-agency almost by design" suggest "coverage creates strong conditions for pseudo-agency"

## 5. Equity Caution

Flag any passage that homogenizes low-income communities or reads as deficit framing:
- "thin participation histories" should be bounded: "many students arrive with thin participation histories"
- "exhausted caregivers" should be bounded: "many caregivers who are exhausted"
- "everyone is lurking" should be bounded: "students may arrive at the same phase simultaneously"
- "low-income families" should be "many high-poverty families"

The book argues that institutional designs presume resources unevenly distributed across households and schools, then interpret the mismatch as individual shortcoming. Do not let revisions slip into the deficit framing the book critiques.

## 6. Doubt Passage

Chapter 13 closes with the honest acknowledgment that the improvement may be in the teaching, the perception, or the population. The doubt is the method, not its failure. Do not let revisions smooth this away or hedge it into certainty. The passage references the author's plan to gather data on student agency in her own classroom.

## 7. IPD Citations

Matusov and von Duyke appears as both 2010 and 2014 in the manuscript. Verify which date is correct for each citation. Lima and von Duyke 2016 is also cited. Check that the claims attributed to these works match what the sources actually say. The IPD critique (internally persuasive dialogue) is central to Chapter 13 and must be attributed correctly.

## 8. Ito Framework

Ito et al. (2010) framework is "hanging out, messing around, geeking out." "Lurking" is Katherine's own term, not Ito's. Do not let revisions attribute "lurking" to Ito. The distinction matters: Katherine uses "lurking" to name a specific developmental phase that precedes "messing around," while Ito's "hanging out" is a broader category of casual social engagement.

## 9. AI Voice Resistance

The AI defaults to the field's pedagogical consensus: engagement as the goal, student voice and choice as the method, direct instruction as suspect. When evaluating AI-generated suggestions, check whether the suggestion pulls the argument toward the field's expectations rather than toward the book's argument. The voice guide is the defense against this tendency. If a suggestion sounds like fluent conventional teacher-talk, it is probably wrong.
