# Decisions ledger for the revision pass

Two kinds of entry. **R-items** are rulings Katherine has made; a lane applies them and never re-argues them. **P-items** are defaults the pass applies until she rules; a lane applies the default, writes `assumes P-nn` in the packet row, and the integrator can find every consequence if she rules the other way. When she rules on a P-item, move it to the R list with the date and leave the old default visible.

The ledger is edited in the repo only. A lane that needs a decision the ledger does not hold adds a P-item at the bottom with a proposed default and carries on.
