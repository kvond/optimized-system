## 11.1 The claim, in your words

## 11.2 The school as a whole answerable

- [C] "It also makes the school as a whole answerable" is the title operating at institutional scale. Three levels: the student answerable for her own word; the teacher answerable to the student's bid; **the school answerable to the students it governs.** The book states the first two; the third is currently nowhere.
- [C] The second justification for democratic agency, harder to dismiss: the developmental argument (students learn deliberation, listening, persuading) is the standard student-voice argument — weak precisely because student councils have made it for fifty years while changing nothing. The **accountability argument** is structural: real student power over shared conditions changes who the institution has to answer to. Different order of claim, and it doesn't depend on whether the skills transfer.
- [C] It inverts the book's own legibility regime: in Ch 4 the sightline runs one way — the institution sees, counts, audits the student. Democratic agency reverses it. Scott turned around — which ties the leadership material to the diagnostic spine instead of leaving it as a separate enthusiasm.

## 11.3 Delivered and declinable, at scale

- [C] Paul got the greenhouse because he took an interest and there happened to be a teacher next door. An independent-study track students must *apply* for selects for exactly the students who arrived apprenticed. The same rule runs at this scale: **delivered and declinable, not merely available** (spine → Ch 7 §4). A slot routinely offered to every junior with a right of refusal reaches students who'd never apply; one requiring a proposal, a sponsor, and a GPA floor concentrates opportunity on the already-advantaged while wearing the language of freedom. Say it in the leadership chapter, or it gets said to you instead of by you.
- From your mapping: **"Save Every Kid" tension** throughout — classroom pedagogy mitigates structure, it does not correct it; what the teacher does with the gap between what they can see and what they can change. **Teacher's Moral Burden** at the close — the teacher can pilot within margin and advocate for structures.

## 11.4 Closer [C]

The classroom chapters end where the classroom's writ ends: pedagogy mitigates structure, it does not correct it. The move this chapter makes is refusing to let that sentence be a resignation — the same principles that govern one room (delivered and declinable; answerability running in both directions) scale to the building, and a school that adopts them becomes answerable to the students it governs.
