# Family Week — August 6–12, 2026
Three people. Six dinners and one free night. Complete [[Kitchen Setup — First Day]] before shopping.

## Thursday Aug 6 — Taco-lime chicken bowls
**Prep 15 min | Cook 25 min**
### Ingredients
- 2 1/4 lb chicken breast, sliced
- [[Marinades]] — Taco-lime scaled for 2 1/4 lb
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 3 cups cauliflower rice
- 1 1/2 cups cooked rice
- 1 avocado
- 3/4 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup cilantro; 2 limes
### Directions
1. Start rice in [[Instant Pot]] or microwave cooked rice.
2. Heat the large [[Cast Iron]] skillet across two burners on medium-high for 5 minutes.
3. Add chicken in two loose zones. Leave untouched 3 minutes; turn and cook 3–4 minutes. Remove at 165°F.
4. Add peppers and onion. Cook 7–9 minutes, stirring only every 2 minutes.
5. Add cauliflower rice for the final 4 minutes.
6. Deglaze with 1/4 cup water and juice 1 lime; scrape browned bits.
7. Plate rice, vegetables, chicken, salsa, avocado, cilantro, lime, and pepper.

## Friday Aug 7 — Ginger beef and broccoli
**Prep 12 min | Cook 22 min**
### Ingredients
- 2 lb lean ground beef
- 24 oz broccoli florets
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 1/2 cups cooked rice; 3 cups cauliflower rice
- Sauce: 1/4 cup tamari, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp ginger, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp arrowroot mixed with 2 tbsp water
- Green onions and sesame seeds
### Directions
1. Heat skillet 5 minutes. Add beef in large pieces; leave untouched 4 minutes.
2. Turn and break into chunks. Cook to 160°F; transfer to a bowl.
3. Add mushrooms and onion; brown 5 minutes. Add broccoli and 1/4 cup water; cover 4 minutes.
4. Add garlic and ginger; stir 30 seconds.
5. Return beef. Add tamari, vinegar, and sesame oil.
6. Stir in arrowroot slurry; cook 1 minute until glossy.
7. Plate over mixed rice and cauliflower rice. Finish with green onions and sesame.

## Saturday Aug 8 — Dijon-dill salmon, potatoes, green beans
**Prep 12 min | Cook 25 min**
### Ingredients
- 2 lb salmon, cut into 3 portions
- 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 1 1/2 lb green beans
- 2 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 lemon, 1/4 cup dill, 2 garlic cloves
### Directions
1. Heat oven to 425°F with the cast iron inside.
2. Microwave potatoes with 2 tbsp water, covered, 6 minutes; drain.
3. Toss potatoes with 2 tsp oil, salt, and pepper. Put cut-side down in hot skillet; roast 10 minutes.
4. Mix Dijon, 1 tbsp lemon juice, zest, garlic, dill, and remaining oil.
5. Turn potatoes. Add green beans and salmon; spread mustard mixture over salmon.
6. Roast 11–14 minutes, checking salmon for 145°F.
7. Rest 3 minutes. Finish beans and potatoes with lemon and fresh pepper.

## Sunday Aug 9 — Free night
Use restaurant, takeout, family choice, or one meal from [[Fast Rescue Meals]]. No planned prep.

## Monday Aug 10 — Red lentil, tofu, and edamame curry
**Prep 15 min | Cook 12 min pressure + release**
### Ingredients
- 3/4 cup dry red lentils, rinsed
- 24 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 24 oz shelled edamame
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp grated ginger; 3 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp curry powder; 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 can light coconut milk
- 1 can 28 oz crushed tomatoes
- 2 cups water
- 6 cups spinach
- Lime and cilantro
### Directions
1. Use [[Instant Pot]] Sauté. Add oil and onion; cook 4 minutes.
2. Add ginger, garlic, curry, and turmeric; stir 45 seconds to bloom.
3. Add lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk, and water. Scrape the bottom completely.
4. Pressure cook High 5 minutes; natural release 7 minutes, then vent.
5. Stir in tofu and edamame. Use Sauté 4–5 minutes until hot.
6. Fold in spinach until wilted. Finish with lime, cilantro, and pepper.
7. Serve without rice; lentils and edamame provide the carbohydrate and fiber.

## Tuesday Aug 11 — Greek lemon chicken and vegetables
**Prep 15 min | Cook 25 min**
### Ingredients
- 2 1/4 lb chicken breast or thighs
- [[Marinades]] — Greek lemon-oregano scaled for 2 1/4 lb
- 2 zucchini, thick half-moons
- 1 red onion, wedges
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 1 1/2 cups cooked quinoa
- 1/2 cup parsley and dill; 1 lemon
### Directions
1. Heat oven to 425°F with cast iron inside.
2. Put marinated chicken in the hot skillet, leaving space between pieces. Roast 10 minutes.
3. Toss zucchini and onion in the remaining marinade bowl; add around chicken.
4. Roast 10 minutes. Add tomatoes; roast 5–7 minutes more.
5. Check chicken at 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice across the grain.
6. Plate quinoa, vegetables, and chicken. Finish with herbs, lemon, and pepper.

## Wednesday Aug 12 — Italian turkey, beans, and zucchini skillet
**Prep 12 min | Cook 25 min**
### Ingredients
- 2 lb ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms
- 2 zucchini, diced
- 24 oz gluten-free dairy-free marinara
- 1 can cannellini beans, drained
- 4 cups spinach
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning; 3 garlic cloves
- Basil and red-wine vinegar
### Directions
1. Heat skillet 5 minutes. Add oil and turkey in large pieces.
2. Leave untouched 4 minutes; turn, break up, and cook to 165°F. Remove.
3. Brown mushrooms and onion 6 minutes. Add zucchini; cook 4 minutes.
4. Add garlic and Italian seasoning; stir 30 seconds.
5. Deglaze with 1 tbsp red-wine vinegar.
6. Add marinara, beans, and turkey; simmer 7 minutes.
7. Fold in spinach. Finish with basil and pepper.

# [[Family Week — Storage and Technique Plan]]
## Refrigerator and freezer map
- Lowest shelf on a rimmed tray: raw taco chicken, salmon, and thawing Greek chicken.
- `Dinner Tonight`: only the next one or two meals.
- `Vegetables Ready`: taco peppers/onion, beef mushrooms/onion, trimmed beans, and washed broccoli in separate labeled containers.
- `Fresh Finish`: cilantro, dill, parsley, basil, green onions, lemons, and limes stored dry and separately.
- Freeze Greek chicken flat until Sunday; freeze turkey until Tuesday if purchased Thursday.
- Store cooked rice and quinoa in shallow containers and refrigerate within 2 hours. Use within 3–4 days or freeze.
- Keep boiled eggs unpeeled. Keep only two shrimp portions refrigerated at a time.

## Cooking techniques used this week
### [[Cast Iron]] and zone cooking
- Preheat the large skillet across both burners for 5 minutes.
- Keep proteins and vegetables in broad zones; visible pan between pieces prevents steaming.
- Rotate the skillet halfway through when one burner runs hotter.

### [[Browning and Deglazing]]
- Taco chicken: leave untouched 3 minutes before turning; deglaze with 1/4 cup water and lime.
- Ground beef and turkey: begin in large pieces and leave untouched 4 minutes before breaking apart.
- Mushrooms: brown before onions so their water does not prevent color.
- Turkey skillet: deglaze with 1 tbsp red-wine vinegar before adding marinara.

### Staggered roasting and par-cooking
- Microwave potatoes with 2 tbsp water for 6 minutes, drain, then roast cut-side down.
- Add dense foods first, quick vegetables later, and tomatoes last.
- Use one layer with visible pan between vegetables.

### Sauce control
- Mix arrowroot with cold water before adding; simmer only about 1 minute until glossy.
- Bloom curry powder and turmeric in oil for 45 seconds.
- In the Instant Pot, scrape the bottom completely before pressure cooking.

### Doneness and resting
- Chicken and turkey: 165°F.
- Ground beef: 160°F.
- Salmon: 145°F.
- Rest chicken 5 minutes, salmon 3 minutes, and whole beef cuts at least 3 minutes.

### [[Fresh Finish]] and [[Plating]]
- Taco bowls: lime, cilantro, avocado, salsa, pepper.
- Ginger beef: green onions and sesame.
- Salmon: lemon, dill, pepper.
- Curry: lime and cilantro after heat is off.
- Greek chicken: parsley, dill, lemon.
- Italian turkey: basil, red-wine vinegar, pepper.
