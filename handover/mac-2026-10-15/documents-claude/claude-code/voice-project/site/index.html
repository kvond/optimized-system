Answerable Teaching

Agency, Perception, and the Ethics of Teaching
Katherine von Duyke, Ph.D. · a working draft, shared for feedback

I teach ninth-grade biology in a high-poverty public school in Delaware, and I am writing a book about what I have learned there: that the crisis we call student disengagement is mostly a crisis of recognition, and that teachers can learn to read the forms of agency students already have. The early chapters are below, a section at a time. This is a working draft, and I would rather have your eyes on it now than later.

Read what interests you. Argue with it. Tell me where it loses you, and where it sounds like your classroom.
