**Subject: A fellow biology teacher who has been quoting you, checking I have you right**

Dear Christian Moore-Anderson,

I teach ninth-grade biology at a high-poverty public school in Delaware, and I am finishing a book called *Answerable Teaching*, about how teachers perceive and respond to the forms student agency takes in a classroom. Your work is in it a lot, and before it goes further I want you to see how, because you deserve to check my use of you against your own meaning. It would be a little ironic to transmit you wrongly in a book that leans on why meaning can't be transmitted.

*Difference Maker* is what introduced me to Variation Theory in practice, and *Teaching Meaning* arrived while I was drafting the teaching chapters and named things I had been circling for years; I think it closes the teaching gap the field has left open. Where you appear: your two problems of biology, the three domains and the causal-dynamic taught mechanistically, frame my chapter on making concepts perceptible. Your principle that meaning can't be transmitted, and that we make a distinction when we perceive a difference that makes a difference, sits under my critical-aspects practice. Your "skilful coping" now anchors a case I call pseudo-agency: a student, Priya, who had come to believe that hearing a lesson was the same thing as learning it. And your convergence/divergence "dance" appears beside Bakhtin's dialogism and Mortimer and Scott's communicative approaches as three vocabularies for one tension. My own wager in the book is a braid of dialogism, Variation Theory, and Ito's phases of engagement; your conversation-plus-variation pairing reached so similar a structure from a different direction that I treat it as corroboration, and I say so in the text.

The working draft is here: [LINK]. The chapters where you appear most are attached, since the public draft currently ends earlier in the book.

May I ask three things, and ask you to skim everything else?

1. Am I representing the two-problems framing and your variation-versus-nonexamples distinction accurately?

3. What are your preferred citations for both books, and are the short quotations, as used, acceptable to you?

Thank you for writing books a working teacher can actually teach from the next morning. It is rarer than it should be.
