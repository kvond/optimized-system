## Structural tics observed across the ten drafts

1. **"It is not X; it is Y" punch with abstract Y** — "Engagement is not the absence of disruption; it is the presence of investment."
2. **First,/Second,/Third,/Finally, paragraph openers** — 5 occurrences (Katherine: 0).
3. **Enumerated-culprit template** — "The first culprit is compliance masquerading as engagement."
4. **Anthropomorphized abstractions** — "the guide whispers that there is no time."
5. **Rule-of-three catalogs everywhere** — "weighing alternatives, justifying choices, monitoring one's own understanding."
6. **Anaphora runs** — "Students learn to perform studenthood: eyes forward, worksheet completed, hand raised…"
7. **Title-case colon headlines** — "Student Agency in the Classroom: What It Is and Why It Matters."
8. **Tidy kicker closes** that restate the essay — "…and come to see themselves as people whose thinking matters."
9. **Generic anecdotes with no named person** — "There is a moment familiar to every teacher: scanning a room of twenty-five faces…"
10. **Balanced antithesis** — "whether they show up in the ways that matter, or simply show up."
11. **"felt sense," "masquerading as," "hidden curriculum of passivity"** — reach-for-profundity noun phrases.
12. **Uniform sentence rhythm** — stdev 8.8 vs her 13.5; almost no fragments, almost no questions.
