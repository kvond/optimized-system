What is wrong with the data

  • Eleven categories carrying $12,754 have no row on the Categories tab, so no budget can attach and no report can group them. That is 204 transactions.
  • ‘Bouldon Brothers’ is budgeted at $100 and shows zero spend, while ‘Boulden's VPI’ carries $3,752 and has no budget. Almost certainly one vendor under two spellings.
  • Three transactions have no category at all, $78.48.
  • One row has no date: ‘Amazon household’, $125. It sits outside every month and every total here.
  • Thirteen pairs share a date, description and amount, including two Amazon Prime charges of $14.99 on April 2. Some are legitimate repeats; the Prime pair looks like a double bill, and a $14.99 refund posted the same day.
  • Four categories show negative months where refunds were booked back into an expense line: Help Kids in February, Amazon unsorted in March, Misc in July, Rental Bills in January.
  • $6,640 of budget sits against categories with zero spending all year. Discover debt is $5,100 of that, and the paydown is real — it is filed as Card payment, so the budget line can never see it.
