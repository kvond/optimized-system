Tank & Flow

A stock is a tank. Flows fill it and drain it. Watch what actually changes the level.

The one thing to notice: the level rises only when inflow is bigger than outflow, falls when it is smaller, and holds steady when they are equal — no matter how large either flow is. Two huge flows that match keep the tank perfectly still. Most people get this wrong; that is the whole point of the model.
