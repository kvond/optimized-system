# Graduate-paper feedback contract

Status: generalized template verified; current course rubric unresolved. Privacy: private operational contract, with no student content. Execution: instructor-reviewed feedback drafting only. This contract enables no model route, schedule, scoring automation, message delivery or grade posting.

Source: UD Claude Scholarly Writing project → View memory → Grading Template, last updated September 11, 2026, read by the primary executor October 12, 2026. [Source project](https://claude.ai/cowork/project/01a02518-24b9-774a-86bf-0912ee705f23).

Maintain distinct instructor and student audiences. Every points decision must cite specific textual evidence. Issues outside the actual rubric may be flagged for instructor judgment but cannot extend grading authority. Never invent a quotation, location, criterion, band, denominator or deduction.

Before a scored draft, verify the readable paper, its assignment/course match, the applicable current rubric and authorized scoring scope. The live generalized template does not supply the actual final-paper rubric, criterion weights or assignment denominator. If these are unavailable, preserve useful evidence-based feedback and leave scoring unresolved for the instructor.

Use these seven sections as the source template’s default:

1. **Header.** Student name, paper, course and date belong in the private feedback artifact when verified. Never put those identifiers or the paper into this repository.
2. **Rubric table.** Criterion, band and points, followed by a total, using the actual applicable rubric. Grammar/Spelling, Content, APA Formatting and Writing are examples from the template; they are not fixed criteria or evidence of current weights.
3. **Genre flag.** An unscored concern for instructor discretion. Apply no unsanctioned deduction for genre fit or other nonrubric issues.
4. **Commendation.** Quote the strongest actual line from the paper and explain why it works. Keep the quotation in the private artifact, never the contract or repository.
5. **Instructor deduction ledger.** For each applicable criterion, identify each exact proposed deduction and supporting textual evidence. Preserve the instructor’s decision authority.
6. **Student-facing feedback.** Give an accessible, constructive version of the same feedback. Do not introduce different scoring claims or new deductions in this version.
7. **Open calls.** Identify scoring decisions to revisit and questions requiring instructor judgment. Leave unresolved calls explicit rather than treating them as approval.

Success means an evidence-grounded draft matched to the verified rubric and reviewed by the instructor. A drafted table or completed chat is not proof that grades were approved, posted or read back. Any downstream delivery or grade entry requires a separately verified and authorized workflow.

The separate WilmU status-pointer workflow remains governed by `skills/block-update/SKILL.md`: status updates do not move, re-status or delete tasks, and do not mutate grading records.
