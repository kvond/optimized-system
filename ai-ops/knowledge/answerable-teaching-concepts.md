Standing as a knower is a relational position in which a person's thinking about a shared disciplinary object can matter to the next move. Agency is the developing capacity to act meaningfully in that relation; it is distinct from motivation, autonomy, participation and choice.

Origination is a contribution arising from the learner's thinking; it need not be correct. Answerability joins responsibility to test/revise one's thinking with the teacher's reciprocal responsibility to engage it. Recognition confirms a contribution as a beginning worth examining.

Intellectual apprenticeship names the practice; intellectual authorship names the developing capacity. The revision ledger specifies “reframing epistemic agency” for the reframing form. Read agency configurations as moments and relationships, never fixed identities.
