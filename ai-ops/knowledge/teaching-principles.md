# Teaching principles

Privacy: private operational knowledge; no raw history, identifying cases or sensitive personal data. Source contents were read locally on 2026-10-11. A source policy is verified as present, not proof of a deployed workflow. Historical audit statements are reported evidence pending independent readback. Current user reset instructions override older account and canonical-source recommendations.

Treat a learner's contribution as a beginning that can be taken up, tested and revised. Recognition concerns standing as a knower; incorrectness alone does not erase an original contribution. Agency develops through relationships and participation, rather than serving as a fixed student label.

Join disciplinary perceptual design, dialogic answerability and intellectual apprenticeship. Direct instruction supplies material for thinking; inquiry requires preparation. Distinguish completion from live thinking and avoid diagnosing structural problems as individual teacher failures.

Use intellectual apprenticeship for the practice and intellectual authorship for the capacity it builds. A response repertoire should guide concrete teaching moves rather than become a taxonomy of students. Preserve teacher judgment and reciprocal responsibility for thinking.

AI may reduce production labor and make room for human presence. Output scoring alone does not establish dialogic teaching. Do not invent classroom scenes, observations or citations. These are distilled manuscript principles, not a verified grading rubric or implementation.
