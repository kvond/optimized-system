# Privacy boundaries

Private GitHub: reusable code, schemas, contracts, distilled operational rules, source pointers and sanitized manifests only. No raw chat, student-identifiable content, health/financial history, family birthdates, credentials, tokens, cookies or full memory dumps. A private repository is not permission to store everything.

Restricted runtime inputs stay in approved private canonical systems and private local/encrypted archive/state. Partition personal, school and scholarly data; cache hashes never replace access checks. Do not grant new connectors or move sensitive data across providers by assumption.

T7 is ExFAT; encryption unverified and Unix permissions are not an access boundary. Recommend an encrypted archive container before sensitive exports. Do not format it or alter hardware. No export requests or sensitive cross-account imports made tonight.
