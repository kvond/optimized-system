## General rules beyond the existing writing-voice bundle

Apply voice markers naturally and selectively, rather than concentrating every characteristic in every paragraph. Gloss a disciplinary term on first use; define both halves of a conceptual contrast. Prefer the field’s actual vocabulary to a metaphor that obscures the term. Preserve the author’s syntax and natural fragments while correcting mechanics; a drafter should not add fragments merely to simulate her rhythm.

Remove machine-added narration of carefulness, chapter-architecture callbacks, roadmap promises, refrains and ornamental summary closers. Short headings should name the subject plainly. Avoid default triplets, repeated sentence openings, abstract antitheses, anthropomorphized abstractions and stock intensifiers or filler praise. Literal uses and the author’s own concrete reversals are exceptions requiring context, not blanket search-and-delete operations. The source’s sparse-dash guidance is approximately three to four per thousand words; it is an editing signal, not a mechanical authenticity detector.

A substantive concern belongs in a bracketed suggestion: duplication between chapters, a missing referent, rhetorical overreach or a challenge to the claim. Do not silently remove the author’s colloquial line or hedge. Distinguish a concrete scale-check from empty meta-commentary. After a cut, check downstream referents and propose any substantive repair.

On every editing pass, verify researcher attribution at the level of the actual term or finding; flag drift with the supporting source. Flag causal language stronger than the evidence and propose a bounded version. Flag broad deficit framing and homogenizing claims rather than extending them.

Where a chapter presents dialogic methods, check that it addresses the conditions enabling a learner’s visibility and willingness to contribute. Keep move names consistent with the current canonical Moves chapter’s headings; historical chapter numbers and sample names are not a current mapping.
